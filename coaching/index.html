Coaching

Coaching is a working partnership. You bring the questions that matter and I bring structure, challenge and support. The aim is always the same, to help you think more clearly and act with greater confidence and purpose.

Who I work with

My clients are senior executives, founders and leaders stepping into bigger roles. Many of them are dealing with genuine complexity, where the stakeholders are numerous, the information is incomplete and the decisions carry real weight. Some are navigating a transition, a promotion, a restructure or a change of direction. Others simply want a confidential space to think with someone who has held demanding roles themselves.

The themes clients bring

  • Making critical decisions when the way forward is unclear
  • Leading a team or an organisation through change and uncertainty
  • Aligning senior stakeholders with different interests and priorities
  • Growing into a larger role and finding the leadership style that fits
  • Building resilience and staying steady under sustained pressure
  • Working out what comes next in a long career

The shape of an engagement

We begin with an initial conversation at no cost. It gives us both a chance to see whether we would work well together, because the relationship matters more than any method.

If we decide to proceed, we agree the focus of the work and how we will know it has been useful. A typical engagement runs for around six sessions over several months, though the shape varies with what you need. Sessions can be in person in London or by video.

Everything we discuss is confidential. Where an organisation sponsors the coaching, we agree at the outset what will be shared and what will not, and the content of our conversations stays between us.

My approach

I trained with Meyler Campbell, whose Mastered Business Coaching Programme is grounded in evidence and practice, and I was awarded a Distinction. My style combines structured thinking with empathy and warmth. I will ask questions that help you see a situation freshly, and where my own experience of leading through complexity is useful, I will share it openly.

I do not arrive with a formula. Every client and every situation is different, and the work follows what you bring.
