Leadership coaching for senior executives and founders

I work with leaders who want to maximise their own impact and the impact of their organisations. My coaching combines structured thinking with empathy and warmth, creating a space where you can achieve clarity, build resilience and act with confidence and purpose.

A career spent leading through change

Before I became a coach I spent more than three decades leading complex business and technology change in the financial services industry. I was a Managing Director at Morgan Stanley for over ten years, where my work ranged from global regulatory transitions to a firm wide migration onto cloud infrastructure. Earlier in my career I helped move a major exchange from open outcry trading to electronic markets.

That experience taught me a great deal about aligning senior stakeholders, holding a team steady under pressure and making decisions when the picture is incomplete. It is the ground my coaching stands on. I know what the roles my clients hold actually feel like from the inside.

How I work

Coaching with me is a confidential, structured conversation held over a number of months. We work on the questions that matter most to you, whether that is a decision you are weighing, a role you are growing into or a period of uncertainty you are leading others through.

I trained with Meyler Campbell on the Mastered Business Coaching Programme and was awarded a Distinction. You can read more about the way an engagement runs on the coaching page.
