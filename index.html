What I do

Coaching built on real experience of leading

Every engagement is confidential, structured and shaped around what you bring. These are the three kinds of work clients most often come to me for.

Executive coaching

One to one coaching for senior leaders in complex organisations. The work focuses on the decisions, relationships and pressures that carry the most weight in your role.

Founder mentoring

A sounding board for founders who are building an organisation around their idea. I bring practical experience of growing teams and delivering under sustained pressure.

Leadership transitions

Support for leaders stepping into bigger roles. The aim is to find the leadership style that genuinely fits you and the confidence to use it from the start.

Background

Three decades leading change in demanding places

Before coaching I led complex business and technology change in the financial services industry. I was a Managing Director at Morgan Stanley for over ten years, led the technology delivery for the LIBOR transition across its sales and trading businesses and ran the programme that moved the firm's applications onto cloud infrastructure. Earlier in my career I helped carry the London futures exchange from open outcry to electronic trading.

I trained as a coach with Meyler Campbell on the Mastered Business Coaching Programme and was awarded a Distinction. You can read the full story on the about page.

Working together

How an engagement runs

01

An initial conversation

We begin with a conversation at no cost, to see whether we would work well together. The relationship matters more than any method.

02

An agreed focus

If we proceed, we agree what the work is for and how we will know it has been useful. Where an organisation sponsors the coaching, we agree at the outset what will be shared.

03

The work itself

A typical engagement runs for around six sessions over several months, in person in London or by video. The content of our conversations stays between us.
