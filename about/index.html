About

I am a leadership coach based in London. I came to coaching after a long career leading large technology organisations in financial services, and I now spend my time helping other leaders do their best work.

The career that came first

I read Mathematics at Cambridge and later took a Master of Science in Knowledge-Based Systems at the University of Edinburgh, back when artificial intelligence was a small academic field rather than a daily headline. I began my working life as a consultant at Accenture, then moved into the markets world that would occupy most of the next three decades.

I held early delivery and architecture roles at James Capel and SG Warburg, and at LIFFE I led projects that helped carry the London futures exchange from the trading floor to electronic markets. After a spell at RBC Capital Markets I joined Morgan Stanley, where I spent sixteen years and served as a Managing Director for more than ten of them.

At Morgan Stanley I led global technology organisations across equities risk, interest rate derivatives and product valuation control. I was the global technology lead for the LIBOR transition across the Institutional Securities Group, a regulatory change programme with a fixed end date and no room for failure. My final executive role there was programme manager for the migration of the firm's applications onto cloud infrastructure across every business globally.

Becoming a coach

Throughout those years the work I found most rewarding was rarely the technology itself. It was helping people think clearly under pressure, preparing a leader for a difficult conversation, or watching someone grow into a role they had doubted they could hold. In time I decided to make that the whole of my work rather than the edge of it.

I trained with Meyler Campbell on the Mastered Business Coaching Programme and was awarded a Distinction. I now coach senior executives and founders at 7A Consulting.

Beyond the day job

I am the operational lead for the Mountain Adventure Fund, a charitable initiative that gives children the opportunity to be inspired by the mountains and the outdoor world. We are part of the Mountain Training Trust, which operates the National Outdoor Centre at Plas y Brenin on behalf of Sport England.

I have also worked in the creative industries on stills shoots, documentary series and independent filmmaking. Those experiences deepened my understanding of collaboration, storytelling and adapting quickly when circumstances change.
